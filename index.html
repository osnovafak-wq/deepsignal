Schedule TikTok videos right from Telegram

Deep Signal Autoposter is a scheduling tool built for the Deep Signal science channel (TikTok @deepsignal.space). The creator sends a video to a private Telegram bot, writes a caption, picks the post settings and the time — and at that time the video is uploaded to TikTok through the official TikTok Content Posting API.

How it works

1Connect TikTok The creator signs in with TikTok (Login Kit) and approves access. The bot shows which TikTok account is connected.
2Send a video Send a video and a caption (up to 2,200 characters, hashtags included) to the Telegram bot.
3Choose settings Who can view the video (no default — the creator always chooses), comments, Duet, Stitch, AI-generated content label, commercial content disclosure, cover frame and the publish time.
4Review & schedule Before scheduling the bot shows a full preview with the TikTok nickname and the Music Usage Confirmation notice. At the chosen time the video is posted (or sent to the TikTok inbox as a draft) and the bot reports the result.

Posting modes

  • Send me the file — at the scheduled time the bot sends the video and caption back to the creator, who posts manually. No TikTok access is used.
  • TikTok drafts — the bot uploads the video to the creator's TikTok inbox (video.upload); the creator finishes and posts it in the TikTok app.
  • Direct post — the bot publishes the video with the creator's chosen settings (video.publish) and sends the link.

Data & privacy

We only use what is needed to post on the creator's behalf: TikTok open ID, display name and access tokens. Videos are kept only until they are uploaded. Nothing is sold or shared with third parties. Access can be revoked at any time with “Disconnect TikTok” in the bot. Read the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
