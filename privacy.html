Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026 · Русская версия ниже

This Privacy Policy explains how Deep Signal Autoposter (“the App”, “we”) handles information when you use our Telegram bot to schedule and publish videos to TikTok. The App is operated by the creator of the Deep Signal channel. Contact: spatikfinal@gmail.com.

1. Information we collect

From TikTok (only after you sign in with TikTok and grant permission)

  • Your TikTok open ID and display name (scope user.info.basic) — to show which account you are posting to.
  • Access token and refresh token issued by TikTok, the list of granted scopes and their expiry times — to upload or publish videos on your behalf.
  • Creator information returned by the Content Posting API (nickname, allowed privacy options, whether comments/Duet/Stitch are allowed, maximum video length) — only to offer you valid posting options. It is not stored permanently.
  • The publish ID and status of each upload, and the public link of a published video.

From Telegram

  • Your Telegram user ID (to restrict the bot to its owner and to send you messages).
  • The videos, captions and post settings you send to the bot, and the scheduled times.

We do not collect passwords, payment details, contacts, location, or analytics/advertising identifiers.

2. How we use information

  • To schedule your posts and, at the time you chose, upload the video to your TikTok inbox or publish it with the caption and settings you selected.
  • To send you reminders, status updates and error messages in Telegram.

We never post anything you did not schedule yourself. We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or training of any models, and we do not sell it.

3. Sharing

Data is sent only to TikTok (to upload/publish your video through the official Content Posting API) and to Telegram (to deliver bot messages). We do not share data with any other third parties.

4. Storage and security

  • Data is stored in a local database on the computer or server where the App runs, which is controlled by the operator.
  • A video file is downloaded only for the upload and is deleted right after a successful upload.
  • Tokens are stored until you disconnect TikTok. All connections to TikTok and Telegram use HTTPS.

5. Retention and deletion

  • Press “Disconnect TikTok” in the bot to delete your TikTok tokens and revoke the App’s access.
  • You can also remove the App’s access in the TikTok app settings (section about apps and permissions / security).
  • To delete all your data (scheduled posts, history), email us — we will delete it within 30 days.

6. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 and is not intended for users under the minimum age required by TikTok.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date above shows the latest version.
